# Technical Workflow

## From Physical Structures to Digital and Interactive Representations

The projects presented in this repository were developed around the transformation of physical architectural environments into structured digital representations.

Although the individual projects used different technologies and had different objectives, the general workflow can be summarized as:

Physical Environment
→
Reference Collection
→
Geometric Interpretation
→
3D Reconstruction
→
Scene Construction
→
Materials / Visualization
→
Interactive Environment
→
Immersive Experience

---

# 1. Reference and Observation

The first stage involved studying the physical architectural structure and identifying its major geometric components.

Complex environments were decomposed into recognizable structural elements such as:

- columns
- arches
- domes
- walls
- roofs
- decorative components
- environmental structures

This decomposition provided the basis for constructing the digital representation.

---

# 2. Geometric Reconstruction

The next stage involved constructing the corresponding 3D geometry.

The goal was to move from visual / architectural reference to an explicit geometric representation.

Conceptually:

Reference
→
Geometric Interpretation
→
3D Structure

The complexity of the final environment depended on the level of architectural detail required by the project.

---

# 3. Scene Construction

Individual geometric components were assembled into complete environments.

This involved organizing:

- architectural structures
- environmental elements
- scene hierarchy
- camera viewpoints
- materials
- textures
- lighting

The result was a coherent digital representation of the physical environment.

---

# 4. Materials and Visualization

Materials and textures were used to provide the visual characteristics required for presentation and interactive environments.

This stage connected geometric structure with visual appearance.

The resulting representation therefore contained both:

- geometric information
- visual information

---

# 5. Real-Time Interactive Environment

The Hafezieh project extended the reconstruction into a real-time interactive environment.

The reconstructed geometry was integrated into Unity.

This enabled:

- real-time visualization
- interactive navigation
- viewpoint changes
- virtual exploration

The important transition was:

Static 3D Representation
→
Real-Time Interactive Representation

---

# 6. Virtual Reality

The Hafezieh project was subsequently extended to virtual reality.

The environment was prepared for Meta Quest, allowing immersive exploration of the reconstructed environment.

The resulting pipeline became:

3D Reconstruction
→
Unity Environment
→
Interactive Exploration
→
VR
→
Meta Quest

---

# 7. Web-Based Exploration

Hafezieh was also prepared for web-based interactive exploration.

This provided another way of presenting the reconstructed environment as an interactive digital space.

---

# 8. Graphics to AI

These projects also provide a useful perspective on my transition from traditional computer graphics toward artificial intelligence.

Earlier work:

Explicitly constructed representations
→
3D geometry
→
Digital environments

Current research:

Learned representations
→
Visual understanding
→
Human motion
→
Embodied intelligence

This progression motivates my interest in the relationship between representation, perception, geometry, and intelligent behavior.

---

# 9. Current Research Connection

The experience gained through explicit 3D reconstruction is relevant to my current interests in:

- 3D computer vision
- representation learning
- spatial understanding
- human-centric representations
- embodied AI
- humanoid robotics
- visual reasoning

A central question motivating this transition is:

How can computational representations of physical environments and human behavior become sufficiently structured and meaningful to support intelligent systems?

This question connects my earlier work in explicit 3D reconstruction with my current work in learned representations and embodied intelligence.
