# Digital Reconstruction & Immersive 3D

## 3D Modeling · Digital Reconstruction · Interactive Environments · Virtual Reality

This repository presents selected projects from my earlier work in 3D modeling, digital reconstruction, interactive environments, real-time visualization, and virtual reality.

The projects presented here include detailed digital reconstructions of:

- Hafezieh — Shiraz, Iran
- Dome of the Rock — Jerusalem

My work in this area involved the reconstruction of complex architectural environments, detailed geometric modeling, scene construction, materials and visualization, interactive environment development, and, in the case of Hafezieh, virtual reality deployment.

These projects form an important part of my technical background in computer graphics and 3D digital environments and provide a foundation for my current research interests in computer vision, representation learning, 3D understanding, and embodied intelligence.

# Selected Projects

## Hafezieh — Interactive 3D Reconstruction & Virtual Reality

Hafezieh was developed as a detailed 3D reconstruction of the Hafezieh complex in Shiraz.

The complete 3D environment was modeled using Autodesk Maya and subsequently implemented interactively using the Unity game engine.

The project was developed as a virtual environment that allowed users to explore the reconstructed site interactively.

A dedicated virtual reality version was also developed for Meta Quest.

The project was additionally prepared for web-based virtual exploration.

# Dome of the Rock — Detailed 3D Reconstruction

This project involved detailed 3D modeling and digital reconstruction of the Dome of the Rock based on the real architectural structure in Jerusalem.

The project focused on detailed geometric reconstruction and architectural modeling, including structural and decorative elements of the environment.

The modeling work was developed using multiple professional 3D content-creation tools.

- Autodesk 3ds Max 2016

- Adobe Photoshop
